Read our story

Brain tumours are more than a medical diagnosis. They affect individuals, families, and entire communities.

Who we are

UO Gray Matter is a student-led initiative at the University of Ottawa dedicated to raising awareness about brain tumours and brain cancer, making scientific knowledge more accessible, and encouraging meaningful engagement with research.

Through education, awareness campaigns, research engagement, and patient and survivor voices, we hope to bridge the gap between science and the community.

What we do

  • Education

    Making scientific knowledge about brain tumours more accessible to everyone, not only those who study it.

  • Awareness campaigns

    Raising awareness about brain tumours and brain cancer at the University of Ottawa and in the wider community.

  • Research engagement

    Encouraging meaningful engagement with the research that moves understanding and treatment forward.

  • Patient and survivor voices

    Listening to the people who live with brain tumours, and to the families and communities around them.

More than awareness

Gray Matter is about building a community that believes in the importance of research, the power of knowledge, and the possibility of a better future.

This initiative is also the first step toward a bigger vision: NeuroHope Canada.
